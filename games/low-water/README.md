# Low Water

A first-person story chapter in Afterlight. The city says Ward Nine was empty
when it opened the floodgates. A call from that night leads you to a station about
to erase its last recording. Follow the call through the intake, optical basin,
and archive, then decide what to send out.

## Play

From the repository root, with Afterlight's normal build dependencies:

```sh
cmake -S . -B build -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build --target run_low_water
```

Use **WASD** and the **mouse** to move and look, **Shift** to move faster, and
**E** to interact. Use the **arrow keys** and **Enter** at the keypad and
transmitter. Press **Esc** to pause or leave a station. The chapter can be
replayed from either ending.

The game requests hardware ray-traced shadows, emissive global illumination, and
water reflections. The optical puzzle uses the reflected image of a physical
sign behind a baffle. **F** at the water sight opens the service plate, which
provides an in-world fallback on raster-only drivers. The `--cinematic` option
adds a second GI bounce, glossy ray reflections, and ray-traced ambient occlusion.
Use `--raster` to explicitly select the raster path, `--window 720x480` to set
the window size, or `--extension /path/to/afterlight_fsr3.so` to use an upscaler.

On macOS the build produces `build/games/low-water/afterlight_low_water.app`.
To use an experimental MoltenVK installation, set `VK_DRIVER_FILES` to its ICD
manifest and invoke the executable under `Contents/MacOS` directly. On Linux
the executable is `build/games/low-water/afterlight_low_water`.

## Source and verification

The level and lighting are in `world.c`. The valve, keypad, calibration board,
and recorder are Blender GLBs generated by `make_models.py`. The signal crown
was modeled and exported with Blender MCP. The WAV files are synthesized by
`make_audio.py`.

```sh
ctest --test-dir build -R '^afterlight.low_water_story$' --output-on-failure
```

The test walks the collision route, exercises the keypad and gate, and checks
both endings. Run the executable with `--verify-ray` on a ray-query driver to
test the hardware path. Add `--capture /tmp/low-water-optics.ppm` to save the
view through the water sight for inspection.
